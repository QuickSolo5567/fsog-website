Editor's note Draft from brief

This edition begins with a question that sits at the centre of every archive: what survives, who gets named and what disappears when no one is left to keep a record?

Across four stories, we return to lives, places and objects that have carried queer memory forward in unexpected ways.

The editors, Fifty Shades of Gay
