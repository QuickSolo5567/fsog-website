Why the archive exists

What history leaves out, communities remember.

Queer history often survives in fragments: a photograph without a name, a letter kept in a drawer, a venue remembered by the people who found safety there.

The Living Archive brings those fragments together through research, storytelling and the people who still carry the memory.
