Our story

More about FSOG

It started with a whiteboard, a marker pen and one question asked at Mumbai's traffic signals: in a fairer world, what right would you want?

  1. 2016

    #IAmNotACriminal

    Back in India after years in international journalism, Shubham Mehrotra started a street photojournalism campaign while Section 377 still made queer love a crime. Trans women, artists, designers and their families stepped in front of the lens.

  2. 8 cities

    From a campaign to a community

    Within months the project ran across eight cities and gathered more than 80 personal stories. As people reached out for help, FSOG added crisis support, legal and mental health guidance, family counselling and campaigns for sex education.

  3. 2026

    Asia's living archive

    After the 2018 ruling decriminalising same-sex relationships, FSOG turned to recovering queer history across South Asia. Today 100,000 people follow along, and The Living Archive goes out free every fortnight.
